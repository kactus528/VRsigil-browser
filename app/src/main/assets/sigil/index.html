How to use it. In the Sigil Trigger browser on your Quest, type sigil.invalid in the address bar to open this page, then press Send. Then play any 360° or 180° video (YouTube works), go full screen and pick 360°. Press A+B on the right controller for the sigil; the video pauses underneath and picks up where it stopped. X on the left controller dismisses it instantly. In any other browser, Send does nothing.
